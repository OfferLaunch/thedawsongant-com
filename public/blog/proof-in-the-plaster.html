Proof in the Plaster: What 100+ Houses Actually Teaches You

By Dawson Gant  /  September 7, 2025

Anyone can model a flip on a spreadsheet. Fewer people can walk a job site at six in the morning and know whether the numbers will survive contact with reality.

Since eighteen I have flipped or built more than a hundred houses across acquisitions, renovations, and ground-up construction. The portfolio in and around Scottsdale is not a highlight reel. It is a ledger of decisions made with drywall dust on your boots.

What the market does not see

They see the finished product. They do not see the foundation inspection that killed a deal before close, the subcontractor swap that saved a timeline, or the Friday night call when a permit stalled and carrying costs started ticking.

That is why I still invest actively. Real estate trained my eye for risk in every other business I touch. If you have never felt a bad foundation under your feet, you will over-trust a model that has never been stress-tested.

  • 100+ houses flipped or built since age eighteen
  • Full-cycle experience — acquisition, renovation, ground-up, and exit
  • Scottsdale focus — disciplined buying in a market that punishes lazy underwriting
  • Operator proof — margins protected by site-level decisions, not post-hoc adjustments

Building houses builds judgment

Construction teaches you to spot the constraint before it becomes a crisis. That skill transferred directly when I scaled agency clients to multiple seven figures per month and when I built telehealth infrastructure under regulatory pressure. Different product. Same requirement: know where the build breaks before your customer feels it.

Dawson Gant is a Scottsdale operator and real estate investor who has flipped or built over a hundred houses. He runs a growth operations practice and a telehealth venture backed by the same hands-on operating standard he learned on job sites.
