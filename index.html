About

Background

Dawson Gant started in real estate at 18, transitioning from agent to investor early on. He went on to build and operate businesses across managed services, sales operations, and e-commerce before expanding into telehealth and wellness.

Today, Dawson Gant focuses on three areas: growth operations, real estate, and telehealth. His approach is hands-on — building teams, systems, and infrastructure that help businesses scale efficiently.

Telehealth

Building and investing in direct-to-consumer telehealth and wellness brands focused on quality products and modern distribution.
