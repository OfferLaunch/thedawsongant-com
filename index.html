About

Building Things That Matter

Dawson Gant is a serial entrepreneur who started his career in real estate at 18 years old. Within three years, he built an eight-figure real estate investment company, then expanded into e-commerce, managed services, and venture investing.

Today, Dawson leads OfferLaunch — a growth infrastructure firm that provides capital, sales operations, and scaling systems for elite operators launching high-ticket offers. His portfolio spans e-commerce operations, health and wellness brands, financial technology, and real estate.

Known for his hands-on operating style and relentless execution, Dawson focuses on building real businesses with real revenue — not hype cycles. His approach is simple: find great operators, give them infrastructure, and get out of the way.
