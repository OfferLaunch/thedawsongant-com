About

Building Things That Matter

Dawson Gant is a serial entrepreneur who started his career in real estate at 18 years old. Within three years, he built an eight-figure real estate investment company, then expanded into growth operations, managed services, and venture investing.

Today, Dawson builds and invests in companies across three core sectors: growth operations, real estate, and telehealth. His approach is hands-on — he doesn't just write checks, he builds the infrastructure, systems, and teams that turn good ideas into scalable businesses.

Known for his relentless execution and bias toward action, Dawson focuses on building real businesses with real revenue — not hype cycles. His thesis is simple: find great operators, give them world-class infrastructure, and scale relentlessly.
